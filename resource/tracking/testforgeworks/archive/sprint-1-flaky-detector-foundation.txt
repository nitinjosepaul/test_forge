SHORT DESCRIPTION
Revised from the original "minimal pytest foundation" sprint. Based on
Sept 22 demo feedback, Sprint 1 now focuses entirely on proving the flaky
detector end-to-end on synthetic data first — pytest/plugin scaffolding
(pyproject.toml, plugins/ folders, core/ promotion) is deferred to
backlog.txt and picked up in a later sprint once the detector logic itself
is proven.
