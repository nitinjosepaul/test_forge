================================================================================
BACKLOG — TestForgeWorks (Kanban)
================================================================================
Unscheduled work, organized as Feature -> Story -> Task so it maps directly
onto the Kanban board:

    Feature   broad product area (e.g. "Flaky Test Detector")
    Story     a slice of work within a Feature (e.g. "Flaky Detector
              Foundation")
    Task      a concrete unit of work sized to 1-2 days. Anything bigger
              is split into multiple 1-2 day Tasks rather than left as one.

Nothing here is pre-assigned to a sprint; Tasks are pulled onto current.txt
deliberately as work progresses. Open Tasks are written in JIRA-import-ready
format:

    Summary: <one liner>
    Description: <as much detail as useful>
    Original Estimate: <1d|2d|TBD>
